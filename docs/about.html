ប្រភពទាំង ៣ · The three sources

Every lesson, quiz answer and chatbot citation in this app points back to one of these three documents.

1 · Khmer

ច្បាប់សាធារណៈអន្តរជាតិ

International Public Law — ឡាយ រត្តនា (Lay Rottana), 2021. 193 printed pages covering the international community, states, sources of international law, the use of force, treaties, conventions and the 1991 Paris Peace Agreements. The full text is searchable in “Ask the book”.

2 · English

Law on the Establishment of the Extraordinary Chambers (ECCC Law)

As amended and promulgated on 27 October 2004 — the legal basis of the Khmer Rouge tribunal in Cambodia, with the Khmer and international practice it introduced.

3 · English

Paris Convention for the Protection of Industrial Property

WIPO text (1883, as revised Stockholm 1967 and amended 1979) — national treatment, the right of priority, patents, marks and the administrative structure of the Union.

+

Cambodian context

The 1991 Paris Peace Agreements, UNTAC and the ECCC are treated in the Khmer textbook itself, so the study path connects general international law with Cambodian practice.

វិធីសាស្ត្រ · How it was built

  1. Text recovery. The textbook PDF uses a legacy Khmer font whose embedded text layer is corrupted. The page text was recovered by mapping each embedded glyph back to the original KhmerOS font (glyph-outline matching) and then converting the display order into proper Unicode logical order. Handy side effect: every quote in this app is the printed text itself.
  2. Structure. Chapters, sections and page numbers come from the book’s own table of contents and section headings — books pages 1–193 (PDF pages 12–205).
  3. Lessons & quizzes. Objectives, key points, terms and questions are written from the recovered text; each item keeps its page reference so you can check it against the book.
  4. Ask the book. Answers are produced by an in-browser search engine (BM25 over the full text, with Khmer-aware character n-grams, because Khmer is written without spaces). It quotes the source and shows the page. It never invents an answer: if the sources do not cover your question it says so. Optionally you can plug in your own AI model key for paraphrased or translated answers.
  5. Everything runs in your browser. No server, no tracking, no account. Your progress, notes and API key stay in this browser’s local storage.

របៀបយោង · How to cite

ឡាយ រត្តនា, ច្បាប់សាធារណៈអន្តរជាតិ (ភ្នំពេញ, ២០២១), ទំព័រ … Lay Rottana, International Public Law (Phnom Penh, 2021), p. …

Each quote card in the app has a “copy citation” button that puts this reference (with the page number) on your clipboard.
